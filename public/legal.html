1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Contact Information

When you create or use a GoRaahi account, we may collect:

  • Mobile phone number
  • Name
  • Email address, if provided
  • Account and login information
  • Information you provide while contacting GoRaahi

Your mobile number may be used for account verification and OTP-based authentication.

1.2 Booking Information

When you search for or make a transportation booking, we may collect information such as:

  • Pickup location
  • Destination
  • Travel date and time

  • Number of passengers
  • Booking details
  • Booking status
  • Information required to communicate with the driver

1.3 Location Information

With your permission, GoRaahi may collect or access your device’s location information.

Location information may be used to:

  • Help identify your pickup location
  • Show relevant transportation services

  • Help connect passengers with drivers

You can control location permissions through your device or browser settings. Some features may not work correctly if location access is disabled.

If you register or apply as a driver, GoRaahi may collect additional information required for driver onboarding and verification, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Address

  • Driving licence information
  • Vehicle registration information
  • Vehicle photographs
  • Identity or verification documents

Driver information may be reviewed for verification and safety purposes.
